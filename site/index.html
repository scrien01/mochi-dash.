This is Mochi.

A little Shih Tzu with a big red topknot, a trusty explorer backpack and one wild idea: see all of New York in a single afternoon.

This is Officer Grubb.

The city's grumpiest dog catcher. He has a net, a whistle and a very personal grudge against fluffy runaways. Keep scrolling. Keep running.

Good thing she has friends.

Rook the courier pigeon swoops in, grabs her backpack and flies her clean away. In this city, the animals look after their own.

Meet the crew

Seven friends, seven powers. Tap a card to see what each one brings to the run, and how you unlock them in the game.

Officer Grubb

Moustache like a broom. Dungarees with a paw-print badge. A ring of keys that jangles every time he gets close. He never stops chasing, and every bump brings him nearer.

Bone Rush

A tiny taste of the big game. Bones are tumbling off the hot dog cart: catch them, dodge the falling crates, and beat your best score.

Drag or tap left and right to move Mochi. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys. You get 30 seconds and three lives.

The city is the level

Every neighbourhood of New York is a new run, with new friends waiting at the end.

How Mochi gets away

Courier Dash

Rook swoops down, grabs Mochi by her backpack and flies her far down the street, right over the obstacles.

Speed ramps

Hit the yellow arrow ramps to launch into the air with a burst of speed and a trail of bones.

Explorer Shield

Mochi's backpack pops up a bubble that shrugs off one bump. Save it for a tight spot.

Crew powers

Unlock friends by beating challenges, then bring one on your run: Lid Bash, Nut Magnet, Charm, Bulldoze or Tiny Turbo.

Race your friends

Pick a room code, send it on WhatsApp, and race on the same street at the same time. Your friends show up as see-through Mochis, with a live leaderboard. No accounts, no sign-in.
